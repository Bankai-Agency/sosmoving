Storage enters a move here for ordinary reasons. Each of those calls for somewhere dry and watched for your things, and for a way to get them there without renting a truck.

With us you never drive to a storage lot or carry a box into a unit. No container sits on your street for days either: whatever the crew loads goes onto our truck and on to the facility, and it is listed on an inventory before it leaves the house.

Three Storage Terms, Matched to Long Beach Calendars

Your dates decide the term. Crew, wrapping and facility are identical across all three.

Short-term storage

A few days up to a few weeks, with no minimum stay. It covers the stretch between handing back one set of keys and receiving the next, a house staged for buyers, or interior work that wraps up within a couple of weeks. We load on the morning you hand back the keys and deliver once you phone in the move-in date.

Long-term storage

A month or longer. Owners use it for a restoration that keeps rooms empty through a season, households moving into a smaller place keep pieces they have not decided on yet, and small businesses park inventory or files. If the project runs past the planned date, the term is extended without any penalty.

In-transit storage

Built for households leaving Long Beach for another state before the destination home is ready. Your things ride with us, wait at our facility and go out to the new address once you have a delivery date, and you avoid hiring a separate storage company at the destination.

What Goes Into Storage and How the Pickup Day Runs

Our facility holds temperature and humidity steady all year. That keeps solid-wood furniture from warping, keeps moisture away from electronics and stops mold from settling into upholstery and linens. The space is monitored and secured for the whole term.

Typical loads include beds and mattresses, sofas and dressers, appliances, TVs and computers, framed art, boxes of holiday decorations, paperwork, and a shop's stock or office furniture. We turn away anything flammable or corrosive, explosives, propane tanks and batteries, perishable food, live plants, and things that draw pests or tend to mildew. Not sure about one item? Ask during the estimate.

Long Beach Storage Pickups: Curbs, Historic Blocks and the 710

Parking & permits: preferential parking districts

Parts of Long Beach are organized as preferential parking districts, where the posted restrictions do not apply to cars that carry a city-issued permit. A household there can hold up to three resident permits and one guest permit a year, and a moving truck is not one of those cars.

So when you book, we ask whether your block sits inside a district and where a truck could stand: a driveway, a stretch of curb with no posted limit, or a spot you have arranged ahead of time. On the day, the crew stages the wrapped pieces near the door and loads in one pass, which keeps the truck at the curb for as short a time as the job allows.

HOA, condo & building rules: historic review before a restoration

In a Long Beach historic district, work on the outside of a house (new windows or doors, repairs, repainting, reroofing, solar) needs a Certificate of Appropriateness before it starts, even when no building permit is required. A second story or an addition over 250 square feet goes further, to the Cultural Heritage Commission. Interior alterations and routine maintenance that change nothing outside skip historic review.

For storage this matters in one way: an exterior project can start later than planned, so we book the pickup around your approval and leave the return date open. When a condo board or management office wants proof of our insurance, the certificate costs $50 and reaches them before pickup day; reserving the elevator or loading area is on your side.

Area specifics: Rose Park bungalows

Rose Park is one of the city's historic districts, a neighborhood of Craftsman bungalow homes built mostly between 1910 and 1922. If you are restoring a house of that age, the furniture needs a dry place to wait while floors, kitchens or porches are redone, and that is when a storage plan earns its keep.

In a century-old house we lay floor runners before the first piece moves, pad door frames and porch posts, and measure large pieces against the older doorways before carrying them. Built-ins stay put; we take only what stands free. When the restoration ends, the return trip follows the same care in reverse, on floors that may have just been refinished.

Logistics & timing: the I-710 in both directions

Every crew starts the day at our Los Angeles base, 5530 Jillson Street. The I-710 is the direct north-south link between the area east of Downtown LA and Long Beach. It follows the Los Angeles River into the city, and its south end splits toward the Port, Downtown Long Beach and the Queen Mary.

A storage job uses that road twice, for the pickup and for the return, often weeks apart, so each leg is booked on its own inside the 8AM – 6PM window, any day of the week. A same-day pickup happens when a crew is open. Returns are smoother with a few days of notice, because the crew locates your inventory at the facility before the truck heads south.

Moving & Storage Questions From Long Beach

Where do my things go after the crew loads them in Long Beach?

Onto our truck and straight to our climate-controlled, monitored facility; no container is left on your street. They stay there, listed on your inventory, until you call with a delivery date.

My house is in a Long Beach historic district. Can storage cover a restoration that is waiting for approval?

Yes. Exterior work in a historic district needs a Certificate of Appropriateness before it begins, so start dates can slide. We pick up when the rooms really have to be empty, and you can extend the term without penalty if the approval or the work takes longer.

What will the pickup and the return cost for a Long Beach address?

Crew time on both trips is hourly: Monday to Thursday, the rate for two movers with a truck begins at $119 an hour in cash, with a 3-hour minimum and a $30 gas fee. Drive time counts double for an address beyond 10 miles of our Los Angeles base, and at 30 miles or more the minimum becomes 4 hours. The storage term itself is quoted at the estimate, once we know how much you are storing.

Can I get one item back before the full delivery?

Yes. Call us and we will either arrange a visit to the facility or bring that item out to you. Name it from your inventory list so the crew can find it quickly.

Can you hold my things if I am leaving Long Beach for another state?

Yes. In-transit storage exists for exactly that: we load in Long Beach, hold everything until the house in the new state can take it, and deliver on the date you give us. The interstate part is priced as a flat rate under USDOT 3398018 and MC 1153871.

Do you pick up on weekends or on the day I call?

Why a Long Beach Move Ends Up With a Storage Stop

Hardly anyone sets out to store their furniture. The call comes when the day you must be out and the day you can get in are not the same day. Everything else about the move stays ordinary, which is why storage works best as one more leg of the move rather than a separate errand you run with a rented van.

In this city the usual gaps look like this:

  • a rental lease that ends before the next landlord hands over the keys;
  • a house emptied for restoration, often in one of the historic districts;
  • a household trading down to a smaller home and still sorting what fits;
  • a job in another state that starts before the house there is ready.

Downsizing is the case people underestimate. A move from a house to an apartment rarely comes with a clear list of what fits, and storage buys the time to decide without selling a dining set in a hurry. Once the new rooms are furnished, we bring over the pieces you picked, and the rest can stay stored or come out on a later trip.

Each case calls for a different term, and each is simpler when the people who wrap your things are the ones who bring them back. That is the core of how SOS runs climate-controlled storage: one company, one inventory and one phone number from pickup to return.

Long Beach Moving and Storage: Choosing the Term

Short-term storage suits gaps you can count in days or weeks. There is no minimum stay and no long commitment, so three nights between two leases are handled as easily as three weeks. Book it when both dates are firm or when the only open question is the exact move-in day.

Long-term storage starts at a month and suits projects with soft end dates: a restoration, a house on the market, a year spent working elsewhere. Pick it when you would rather not re-book every few weeks. You can extend at any point without a penalty, so there is no need to guess the full length on the first day.

In-transit storage belongs to interstate moves. The load leaves the city with us, waits at our facility and goes to the destination when the new home can take it. If that sounds like your plan, our team for out-of-state moves from Long Beach quotes the road portion as a flat rate, and the storage stretch becomes part of the same plan.

Small businesses use the same three options. A shop clearing its back room for a remodel, or an office holding files while it settles into a smaller suite, books long-term storage and asks for single boxes as needed.

Torn between two terms? Start with the shorter one. Adding time later costs a phone call, not a fee.

Getting the House Ready Before the Pickup Crew Arrives

A storage pickup moves faster when the rooms are ready. The crew brings blankets, shrink wrap, padding and wardrobe boxes, but the sorting is yours: what goes to storage, what goes with you to the next address and what stays behind for good.

  • Keep one room or corner for the things that travel with you, and tell the crew so nothing from it gets wrapped.
  • Set aside what the facility cannot take: fuel and propane, solvents, batteries, food and houseplants.
  • Flag anything you may want during the term, such as tax files or a winter coat, so it is easy to find on the inventory.
  • Keep hallways and the route to the front door free of boxes and rugs, and mention steps, gates or a narrow side yard when you book.

If you would like the boxing done for you as well, our packing help for Long Beach homes can handle the kitchen, books and closets before the truck is loaded. Boxes run from $4 each, billed only for those the crew actually fills, while tape and stretch wrap carry no charge.

Take photos of anything with loose parts, such as a bed frame or a desk with shelves, before it is taken apart. Pictures also help you decide where each piece should stand when it comes back.

Restoring a Historic Home With the Rooms Empty

Long Beach protects its historic districts through a review process, and that process shapes a storage plan more than most owners expect. The empty-house date depends on paperwork as much as on the contractor, because exterior work cannot begin until the Certificate of Appropriateness is issued.

Scale changes the calendar too. A second story or an addition over 250 square feet is heard by the Cultural Heritage Commission, and a hearing brings its own schedule. Projects kept entirely indoors, with nothing changing on the outside, avoid historic review, so those are the easiest to time.

Our advice is to set the pickup for when the contractor truly needs the rooms, not for the day you apply. Start with long-term storage if the project touches the exterior, and keep the return open. When the crew comes back, it puts floor protection down again, since newly refinished floors scratch more easily than anything else in the house.

Neighborhoods such as Rose Park, filled with Craftsman bungalows in the 1910s and early 1920s, are the kind of place where this sequence plays out. Tell us which rooms the work reaches first; we can clear those and leave the rest for a second visit if that suits the project.

Moving and Storage in Long Beach, CA: Two Trips, One Plan

A storage job is really two moves separated by time. The first trip carries everything from your home to our facility; the second takes it to the next address, which may be across town, in a neighboring city or in another state. Each trip is scheduled separately within our 8AM – 6PM working day, weekends included.

Pick up early in the week if the dates allow: Monday to Thursday rates are lower than Friday to Sunday for every crew size. For the return, call when the move-in date is firm and give us a few days. When you need a pickup on short notice, ask anyway, because same-day jobs are possible whenever a crew is free.

Parking deserves a thought on both ends. If either address is on a block inside a preferential parking district, let us know at booking and we will work out where the truck will stand, and if the new building has a loading area or elevator, reserve it for the delivery day as well as for the pickup.

Both trips use the I-710 between our Los Angeles base and the city, and the travel rules work the same way in each direction. If the next home is also in town, the return is a plain local delivery, and our local crews for Long Beach moves can quote both ends together.

Where the Hours Go on a Storage Job

Crew time is the biggest variable. It grows with the number of pieces, the stairs and the walk between the front door and the truck, and it shrinks when boxes are packed, rooms are sorted and a parking spot is waiting. A crew of four costs more per hour yet often finishes a large house in fewer hours than two movers would.

The return trip can run quicker than the pickup, because everything is already wrapped and listed. Most of that time goes to carrying pieces in, unwrapping them and setting furniture where you want it.

Fixed charges are few and known in advance: the $30 gas fee, a $50 deposit credited to the bill, a $50 insurance certificate when the building requires one, and $200 for a second truck when one is not enough. Pieces heavier than 300 lbs carry a $150 charge and need a crew of three.

The storage term is the other half of the price, set by the space your inventory fills and by the length of the stay. We give you that number at the estimate. To weigh a combined service against renting space and hauling it yourself, see our article on combining a move with storage in Los Angeles.
