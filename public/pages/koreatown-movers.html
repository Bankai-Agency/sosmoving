Local Moving From Koreatown Across LA

Our local crews move Koreatown households anywhere in Los Angeles by the hour, and our long-distance movers take over when the new address is out of state. The city draws Koreatown's official lines at Vermont Avenue, Western Avenue, Third Street and Olympic Boulevard, west of Downtown LA and south of Hollywood. Rates start from $119/hr for two movers and a truck Monday–Thursday at the cash rate, and $135/hr Friday–Sunday.

What we plan for on Koreatown streets

  • Wilshire bus lane: Monday–Thursday during rush hours the Wilshire curb lane through Koreatown is reserved for buses, and cameras on Metro buses ticket vehicles parked in it, so we schedule the truck stop around those windows.
  • Street parking: Parking is hard to find in Koreatown, especially in the evening, so we ask about your loading area, garage entrance or driveway when you book.
  • Any building: Whether home is a tower by Wilshire/Vermont, where the Metro B and D lines meet, a unit near the D Line's Wilshire/Normandie or Wilshire/Western stations, or an older building off the boulevard, stairs, long carries and multiple stops stay in the hourly rate.
Longer local routes can fall under CPUC Double Drive Time rules, while long-distance and out-of-state moves are priced through a free estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you move me into a Koreatown high-rise that asks for a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Send us your property manager's requirements when you book, and we'll prepare the certificate of insurance before move day and plan the job around your reserved elevator time. SOS Moving is CPUC licensed and carries full liability coverage and workers' compensation.

Where does the moving truck park for a move on Wilshire Boulevard?

Monday–Thursday during rush hours, the curb lane on Wilshire through Koreatown is reserved for buses, and Metro buses carry cameras that ticket vehicles parked in it. We plan the truck stop outside those windows, or around your building's loading area if it has one. Street parking in Koreatown is hard to find, especially in the evening, so share your building's access details when you book.

What do movers in Koreatown, Los Angeles charge per hour?

Rates start from $119/hr for two movers and a truck Monday–Thursday at the cash rate, and $135/hr Friday–Sunday. Stairs, long carries and multiple stops are included; a $30 gas fee applies, and the $50 deposit goes toward your total cost. Pianos add $200, and items heavier than 300 lbs add $150.

Can you do a same-day move in Koreatown?

Same-day moves are available based on crew availability. Call 909-443-0004, Monday – Sunday, 8AM – 6PM, and mention whether your building needs an elevator reservation or a certificate of insurance, since those can decide how soon we can start.

Do you move people from Koreatown to other states?

Yes. Our long-distance service handles out-of-state and cross-country moves from Koreatown, and packing, storage and white glove handling can be added to the same move. Request a free estimate with both addresses and your move date.

How early do I need to schedule my moving date?

Book as early as you can. Weekends and the end of the month fill up first, and long-distance moves need extra time for planning. For an urgent move, call us: same-day crews are often available.

Do you move on weekends and holidays?

You can choose any convenient day for your relocation, and yes, we can help you move on weekends and holidays.

What are the items I can not pack?

A safe relocation is our goal, so we’ll provide you with a detailed list of items that are prohibited to transport. It includes perishables, hazardous materials, firearms, batteries, etc. We also don’t recommend packing your personal and irreplaceable items, including family heirlooms, family photos, jewelry, documents, etc. Carry them with you during the move.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, and certified checks. We don’t accept personal checks. There will not apply any transaction fee on card transactions.

Will you call me before delivery?

Yes, we’ll contact you prior to delivery. We’ll deliver your belongings within the given moving arrival window. In case the window is no longer possible, we’ll also contact you in advance and set another delivery time.
