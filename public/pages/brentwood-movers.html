White Glove Moving for Art, Antiques and Fine Furniture

Brentwood is home to the Getty Center, and your own collection deserves the same patience on moving day, whether it is one framed canvas or a house full of antiques. Our white glove movers bring that care to every job, from a Period Revival house in Brentwood Park to a mid-century modern home in Crestwood Hills. Comparing Brentwood luxury movers? Ask each one exactly how they wrap a framed painting; here is our answer.

How our white glove handling works:

  • Wrapped item by item: Blankets, shrink wrap and tape go on every piece, framed art and mirrors get extra padding, and hanging clothes travel in wardrobe boxes.
  • Taken apart, rebuilt: Beds, dining tables and oversized furniture are disassembled for the trip and reassembled in the new rooms, and wall-mounted TVs come down before loading.
  • Placed where it belongs: The crew sets each piece where you point and helps you unpack, so the house works on the first night.
Want the rest of the house boxed too? Add packing services and the crew packs books, kitchenware and closets before the truck is loaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you move us into a gated community like Brentwood Circle?

Yes. Brentwood Circle is gated, and Brentwood Country Estates in lower Mandeville Canyon is guard-gated, so send us the gate instructions and any HOA requirements for movers when you book. We set the arrival window around them so the truck is not left waiting at the entrance.

What does an hour of white-glove crew time cost in Brentwood?

Rates start at $119/hr for 2 movers and a truck Monday–Thursday at the cash rate, and $135/hr Friday–Sunday. There is a $30 gas fee, and the $50 deposit is applied toward your total. Stairs, long carries and multiple stops are included; a piano adds $200 and any item heavier than 300 lbs adds $150.

Do you offer white glove service for art and antiques in Brentwood?

Yes. Our white glove service covers blanket and shrink-wrap protection, furniture disassembly and reassembly, TV unmounting, placement and unpacking help. List your most valuable or oversized pieces when you book so we can plan the load around them.

Can you move a condo on San Vicente or Wilshire?

Yes. Most of Brentwood's apartments and condos sit between San Vicente and Wilshire or along Barrington Avenue, and our apartment movers work around building schedules. If your building reserves the elevator or loading area for moves, book the slot and send us the time window.

Is this Brentwood in Los Angeles or in Contra Costa County?

This page covers Brentwood on the Westside of Los Angeles, bounded by I-405 on the east and Santa Monica on the southwest. There is also a city of Brentwood in Contra Costa County, in the East Bay. A move between the two is a long-distance move, and we quote it like any other.

How early do I need to schedule my moving date?

Book as early as you can. Weekends and the end of the month fill up first, and long-distance moves need extra time for planning. For an urgent move, call us: same-day crews are often available.

Do you move on weekends and holidays?

You can choose any convenient day for your relocation, and yes, we can help you move on weekends and holidays.

What are the items I can not pack?

A safe relocation is our goal, so we’ll provide you with a detailed list of items that are prohibited to transport. It includes perishables, hazardous materials, firearms, batteries, etc. We also don’t recommend packing your personal and irreplaceable items, including family heirlooms, family photos, jewelry, documents, etc. Carry them with you during the move.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, and certified checks. We don’t accept personal checks. There will not apply any transaction fee on card transactions.

Will you call me before delivery?

Yes, we’ll contact you prior to delivery. We’ll deliver your belongings within the given moving arrival window. In case the window is no longer possible, we’ll also contact you in advance and set another delivery time.
