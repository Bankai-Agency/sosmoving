**Yes, movers work on weekends.** Most moving companies work weekends, though schedules vary from company to company. SOS Moving works Monday through Sunday, 8AM–6PM. What changes on a weekend is the price and how fast slots go: our Friday–Sunday rate is $16 an hour higher than Monday–Thursday, for every crew size.

That last part catches people off guard. For our pricing, the weekend starts on Friday, not Saturday. So if the plan is to take Friday off and get the move done before the weekend, you pay the same hourly rate as on Sunday. Thursday is the last day of the week at the lower rate.

## Do moving companies work on weekends and Sundays?

The weekend is when most people want to move. They work Monday to Friday, leases tend to end at the end of the month, and a weekend move means nobody burns a vacation day. Moving companies know this, which is why practically all of them schedule weekend crews.

What varies is the schedule. Not every company works every day of the week, and hours differ, so if you're comparing movers, ask the plain question: do you work on the Saturday or Sunday I need, and from what time to what time? Don't assume.

At SOS the hours don't change on weekends. Saturday and Sunday crews work 8AM to 6PM, the same as on a Tuesday. The move has to fit inside that window, so a full house is better started at 8AM than after lunch.

We also take moves on holidays. Ask for the rate for holiday dates when you book.

### Saturday or Sunday?

For our crews the two days are the same: the same Friday–Sunday rate and the same 8AM–6PM hours. Which one is open depends on the schedule for that weekend, so ask about both when you call. Two things outside our schedule can still decide it. Some apartment complexes and condos limit moving hours on Sundays or don't allow Sunday moves at all, so check with the building first. And in the City of Los Angeles one curb rule treats the two days differently, which we cover below.

## Do movers charge more on weekends? Our rates, day by day

Some companies charge more for weekend dates, some don't, and the way they do it varies. The only reliable answer is the rate for your exact day, in writing. Here's how ours works. Local moves are billed by the hour, and the hourly rate depends on two things: the day of the week and the size of the crew.

|---|---|---|
| 2 movers | $119 / $129 | $135 / $145 |

Each additional mover adds $40 an hour. Look across any row and the gap between the two halves of the week is the same: $16 an hour. Paying by card adds $10 an hour on any day. One note from the same table: rates may vary at the beginning and end of the month, so confirm the rate for your exact date when you book.

A few rules stay the same all seven days:

- 3-hour minimum, or 4 hours when an address is 30+ miles from our base.

- A flat $30 gas fee, charged once per move.

- A $50 deposit to book. It counts toward the final bill, it isn't added on top.

- After 8 hours of work, overtime is billed at 1.5× the hourly rate: for two movers on a Saturday paying cash, $135 × 1.5 = $202.50 an hour.

### What the weekend difference looks like in dollars

| Example | Monday–Thursday | Friday–Sunday | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 movers, cash, 3-hour minimum | 3 × $119 + $30 gas = $387 | 3 × $135 + $30 gas = $435 | $48 |
| 2 movers, cash, 4 hours | 4 × $119 + $30 gas = $506 | 4 × $135 + $30 gas = $570 | $64 |
| 3 movers, card, 5 hours | 5 × $169 + $30 gas = $875 | 5 × $185 + $30 gas = $955 | $80 |

So yes, it is cheaper to move Monday through Thursday. But notice the size of the gap: $48 to $80 in these examples. Whether that's worth a day off work is your call, and for a lot of people it isn't.

What really inflates a weekend bill is time, not the rate. A slow elevator, a truck parked half a block away, boxes still being packed when the crew walks in. One extra hour for two movers on a Saturday costs $135 in cash. That's more than the whole weekend difference on a short move.

Weekends and the last days of the month usually fill first, so book as early as you can. Most people are free on the same two days, and leases often turn over at the end of the month, so a lot of moves compete for the same dates.

## Weekend moving in Los Angeles: curb, building, clock

### Yellow curbs on Saturday vs Sunday

According to [LADOT's colored curb rules](https://ladot.lacity.gov/residents/colored-curb-zones), yellow curbs in the City of Los Angeles are commercial loading zones Monday through Saturday, 7AM to 6PM, unless a sign says otherwise. During those hours a vehicle with commercial plates may stay there for up to 30 minutes while it's actively loading or unloading. Outside those hours, the other parking rules for that curb apply.

Two things follow from that. Saturday is not a weekend for yellow curbs: the 30-minute limit works the same as on a Tuesday, and 30 minutes rarely covers a whole apartment. On Sunday the yellow-zone limit itself isn't in effect unless it's posted, but every other sign on the block still is. Walk the block the day before and read what's actually posted. And if you're moving in [Santa Monica](/los-angeles-movers/santa-monica-movers), [Burbank](/los-angeles-movers/burbank-movers) or another separate city inside LA County, that city's own curb rules apply, not LADOT's.

### Temporary no-parking signs

If you want to hold the curb in front of your building, the request is yours to file: you apply for the temporary no-parking signs, and we'll tell you which section of curb to request for the truck. Start early in the week, not on Saturday morning. [LADOT's FAQ](https://ladot.lacity.gov/about/faq) sends questions about temporary no-parking signs to its Special Traffic Control Division, (213) 485-2298.

### Building rules

Apartment and condo buildings set their own move rules, weekends included. Before you book, ask management four things: are moves allowed on Saturday and on Sunday, during which hours, do you need to reserve the service elevator or loading dock, and what paperwork do they want from the moving company. Share the answers when you book, so the crew arrives when your elevator window opens.

### The 8-to-6 clock

Our crews work 8AM to 6PM every day. On a weekend that's worth planning around: take the morning start for anything bigger than a studio, and have everything packed before the crew walks in, either by you or with [packing help](/services/packing-services) booked ahead. If the new place won't be ready on your moving day, [storage](/services/storage) can bridge the gap instead of pushing the move to another weekend.

## How to keep a weekend move short

- **Finish packing first.** On an hourly bill, packing time on a Saturday is billed at the Saturday rate.

- **Reserve the elevator and plan the curb.** Carrying from half a block away adds time to every trip.

- **Pay cash if you can.** Two movers on a Saturday cost $135 an hour in cash and $145 by card.

- **Look at Thursday before Friday.** Same day off from work, $16 an hour less.

- **Tell us everything when you book.** Floors, stairs, elevator, both addresses. Getting the crew size right the first time matters more than the rate.

## Weekend moving FAQ

### Do movers work on Sundays?

Many do, but schedules vary from company to company, so ask each one directly. SOS Moving works Sundays from 8AM to 6PM, with the same hours and the same Friday–Sunday rate as Saturday. Whether a Sunday slot is open depends on the schedule for that date.

### Do movers work on holidays?

Some do, so it's worth asking. SOS Moving takes moves on holidays too. Ask for the rate for holiday dates when you book.

### Is Friday a weekend day for moving rates?

With us, yes. Our Friday–Sunday rates are $16 an hour higher than Monday–Thursday. Other companies split the week differently, so ask which days their weekend rate covers.

### Do moving companies charge more on weekends?

Many do. Ours: two movers with a truck cost $135 an hour in cash Friday–Sunday, against $119 Monday–Thursday. The same $16 gap applies to three and four movers.

### Is it cheaper to move on a weekday?

Monday through Thursday, yes. A 4-hour move with two movers paid in cash comes to $506 on a Thursday (4 × $119 + $30 gas) and $570 on a Friday or Saturday (4 × $135 + $30 gas).

### Can I book a same-day move on a weekend?

Yes, when a crew is free. Call between 8AM and 6PM; a request late in the afternoon may become a booking for the next morning, because the job has to fit into the working day.

### Do you move in the evening on weekends?

No. Our crews work 8AM to 6PM every day of the week, weekends included.

### What's the minimum charge on a weekend?

The same as any other day: 3 hours, or 4 hours when an address is 30+ miles from our base, plus a $30 gas fee. For two movers paying cash on a Saturday, the minimum is 3 × $135 + $30 = $435. The $50 deposit counts toward that total.

### Do I need to book a weekend move further ahead?

Usually, yes. Weekends and the last days of the month tend to fill first, so book as early as you can. And confirm the rate for your exact date: rates may vary at the beginning and end of the month.
